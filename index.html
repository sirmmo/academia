Research

Open History Map

An open, collaborative platform for historical and archaeological geodata, mapping the past through time and space. Running since 2014.

Digital archaeology & heritage

WebGIS for cultural heritage, contextualising 3D model archives, quantitative studies of Roman entertainment buildings, participatory public history.

Open knowledge

Wikipedia and wiki platforms in archaeology, Semantic MediaWiki data integration, open-data ecosystems and edutainment.

Smart cities & digital twins

City sensing from electric micromobility traces and interconnected urban networks through GlassBox digital twins, in collaboration with the University of Bologna.

Geo-aware AI

Semantic grounding for geospatial AI agents (GeoBard) and knowledge representation for historical data.

    doi:10.19282/ac.35.2.2024.07

    Simplifying contextualization of 3D model archives in webGIS: 3DModelCommons

    Montanari, M., Marsicano, L. · Archeologia e Calcolatori

    doi:10.19282/ac.35.2.2024.27 
    @article{montanari_marsicano_2024, address={IT}, title={Simplifying contextualization of 3D model archives in webGIS: 3DModelCommons}, volume={35}, ISSN={1120-6861, 2385-1953}, url={https://doi.org/10.19282/ac.35.2.2024.27}, DOI={10.19282/ac.35.2.2024.27}, abstractNote={This paper presents a transformative approach that allows for the utilization of existing 3D models from diverse sources within a geographic context. It introduces the concept of external metadata, which describes these models, making them searchable, accessible, and seamlessly intergrated in webGIS environments using Three.js and MapLibre GL. This paper addresses the demand for the reuse of three-dimensional data representation in the geospatial domain and acknowledges the wealth of 3D models available from various sources. By introducing a standardized metadata schema, it establishes a structured framework for the incorporation of these models into webGIS systems. A central theme of this work is the development of a metadata standard that acts as a bridge between 3D models and webGIS environments granting it all information that can be used to correctly locate, scale and orient the models. It enables efficient searching, rendering, and utilization of these models within geographic contexts.     Open History Map

    Bernardoni, S., Montanari, M., Trojanis, R. · Archeologia e Calcolatori

    doi:10.19282/ac.28.2.2017.44 
    @article{bernardoni_montanari_trojanis_2017, address={IT}, title={Open History Map}, volume={28}, ISSN={1120-6861, 2385-1953}, url={https://doi.org/10.19282/ac.28.2.2017.44}, DOI={10.19282/ac.28.2.2017.44}, abstractNote={OpenHistoryMap aspires to become the open source geographical system for archaeological information, both from an academic and an educational point of view.
